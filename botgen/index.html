How it works

Three steps from idea to live bot

BotGEN removes every technical barrier between your idea and a working Telegram bot.

1

Chat with the constructor

Open the BotGEN constructor on Telegram and describe what your bot should do in plain language — no jargon required.

2

AI assembles your bot's config

Claude by Anthropic interprets your description and generates a complete bot configuration — flows, responses, and logic.

3

Your bot goes live instantly

The BotGEN engine deploys your bot via webhooks. Your Telegram bot is live and responding to users within seconds.
